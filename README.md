**YokaiVerse** is an all-in-one platform designed to bring **Anime, Manga, and Webtoons** together in a single, seamless experience. Built with a "Privacy First" mindset, this project stands against data exploitation, ensuring your digital footprint remains yours and yours alone.

---

## 🛡️ Privacy & Security (Zero-Knowledge)

I believe privacy is a fundamental right. YokaiVerse is developed with a **Zero-Knowledge Architecture**:

- **Client-Side Encryption:** Your preferences, watchlists, and history are encrypted directly on your device using your unique encryption key.
- **No Server Access:** We do not (and cannot) see your private data. Everything stored on our database is an encrypted blob that only you can unlock.
- **Your Key, Your Responsibility:** Since your data is encrypted with a key derived from your password, resetting a lost password will result in the permanent loss of previous encrypted data.

## 🚀 The Roadmap

1.  **Web & API Integration:** Transitioning from local to a secure web-supported structure.
2.  **Performance & UI/UX:** Faster and cleaner interface than mainstream alternatives.
3.  **Multilingual Support:** Starting with **Turkish** and **English**, expanding based on community growth.
4.  **The "3-in-1" Experience:** Anime, Manga, and Webtoons in one unified dashboard.
5.  **BYOK (Bring Your Own Key):** Users can use their own API keys for AI features to maintain total control.

## 💰 Sustainability & Support

Since I don't sell your data or use intrusive ads, you might wonder how this project stays alive. The answer is simple: **It depends on you.** We offer three tiers of support:

- **Free Tier:** Access to the platform with reasonable limits. Designed for casual users to enjoy the content without being overwhelmed.
- **Premium Tier:** Removes most limitations. (Note: This is still in development, so the "almost" is important here!)
- **Supporter Tier:** This is essentially the Premium tier but at a higher price point. It offers no extra features other than a unique **Supporter Badge**. Its sole purpose is to provide a way for those who truly believe in the project to donate and help keep the servers running.
